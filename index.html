How amounts are calculated

Earned revenue is a valid, non-cancelled agreed sale amount (VIP or advertising) or finalized monetization earnings. Cash received is the valid amount actually paid. Outstanding receivables is agreed price minus received cash.

Estimated earnings are provisional monetization records only and are excluded from earned revenue. Pending payouts are finalized monetization earnings minus their amount received. Currency totals stay separate; this application never adds MMK, THB, and USDT together.
